Why does it take Ethereum to transfer DLUX?

DLUX is an ERC-20 token. This means that is does not run on a proprietary blockchain (a system of verifying transactions, mining, etc.), so it must operate under a larger one (the Ethereum blockchain). Ethereum is needed to pay for DLUX transactions and only Ethereum addresses can recieve DLUX.
